The Fields Medal

The Fields Medal is one of the most prestigious awards in mathematics. Named after John Charles Fields, a Canadian mathematician who championed its creation, it was first awarded in Oslo in 1936. The medal is presented every four years at the International Congress of Mathematicians.

The Age Rule

A candidate's 40th birthday must fall on or after January 1 of the award year. This rule ensures that recipients are recognized for their work while still having time to contribute further to mathematics.
