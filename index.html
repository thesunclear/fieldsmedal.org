Latest Medalists

2026

  • Yu Deng
    Partial differential equations
    He showed how the Boltzmann equation for a dilute gas follows from the motion and collisions of individual particles.
  • John Pardon
    Symplectic geometry and topology
    He developed new ways to count holomorphic curves, a central tool for studying symplectic spaces.
  • Jacob Tsimerman
    Arithmetic and algebraic geometry
    He used o-minimality to study problems in arithmetic geometry and helped prove the André–Oort conjecture for Siegel modular varieties.
  • Hong Wang
    Harmonic analysis and geometric measure theory
    She made major advances on the three-dimensional Kakeya problem, which asks how small a set can be if it contains a line segment in every direction.
