Maryna Viazovska: Sphere Packing in Eight Dimensions

Packing Balls Efficiently

The problem of packing identical balls as densely as possible is a classic challenge. In two dimensions, the densest packing is achieved by arranging circles in a hexagonal lattice — an arrangement that can be visualized and explained without advanced mathematics.

The E8 Lattice

Maryna Viazovska proved that the E8 lattice achieves the maximum density among all packings of equal balls in eight-dimensional space. This result solved a long-standing problem in geometry and coding theory.

Proving that no other packing can achieve a higher density was the difficult part — requiring deep insights into harmonic analysis and modular forms.
